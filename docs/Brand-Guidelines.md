# 🎨 Color Usage Rules

## 🔵 Primary Blue (`#0653A5`)

**Purpose:** Represents trust, professionalism, and reliability.

**Use for:**

* Logo
* Main Navigation
* Primary Headings (H2, H3)
* Primary Buttons
* Icons
* Links
* Active Navigation Item
* Section Titles

**Avoid:**

* Large background sections
* Paragraph text
* Footer background

---

## 🟢 Healing Green (`#5AA213`)

**Purpose:** Represents healing, wellness, growth, and recovery.

**Use for:**

* Book Appointment Button
* WhatsApp Button
* Call-to-Action Sections
* Statistics
* Success Messages
* Check Icons
* Highlights
* Feature Icons

**Avoid:**

* Long paragraphs
* Main headings
* Footer background

---

## ⚪ White (`#FFFFFF`)

**Purpose:** Creates a clean, spacious, and professional medical appearance.

**Use for:**

* Cards
* Forms
* Buttons (secondary)
* Section Backgrounds
* Content Containers
* Input Fields

---

## 🌫️ Background (`#F5F6F8`)

**Purpose:** Soft neutral background that reduces eye strain.

**Use for:**

* Website Background
* Alternate Sections
* Feature Areas
* Testimonial Backgrounds

---

## ⚫ Navy Blue (`#06172E`)

**Purpose:** Strong contrast for important information.

**Use for:**

* Top Information Bar
* Footer
* Contact Details
* Copyright Area

**Avoid:**

* Main page backgrounds
* Large content sections

---

## ⚫ Charcoal (`#2F2F2F`)

**Purpose:** High readability.

**Use for:**

* H1 Headings
* Important Text
* Card Titles

---

## 🌫️ Medium Gray (`#626263`)

**Purpose:** Secondary content.

**Use for:**

* Paragraph Text
* Descriptions
* Labels
* Supporting Information

---

## 🌿 Light Blue (`#EAF4FF`)

**Purpose:** Gentle emphasis without overwhelming the user.

**Use for:**

* Alternate Sections
* Information Cards
* Highlight Blocks

---

## 🌱 Light Green (`#EEF8E7`)

**Purpose:** Reinforces the healthcare theme.

**Use for:**

* Feature Cards
* Benefits Section
* Success Areas
* CTA Backgrounds

# Design Principles

-   Keep generous white space.
-   Use rounded corners (12--16px).
-   Use subtle shadows.
-   Follow a clean healthcare aesthetic.
-   Maintain accessibility with sufficient color contrast.
